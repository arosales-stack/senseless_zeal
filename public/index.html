About

A recurring contradiction runs through the work: fragility and grotesqueness, innocence and morbidity, order and chaos, tenderness and unease.

The imagery draws on gothic romanticism, surrealism, memento mori, expressionist distortion and the directness of naïve and outsider art, held together by one personal graphic vocabulary: stippled bodies, hatched limbs, long hair, bones that dance.

Death, anatomical distortion and psychological discomfort sit beside grace, humour, affection and delicacy. Darkness is not staged as spectacle or threat. It is an ordinary part of an imagined world, populated by vulnerable, eccentric and strangely living figures.

I'm Audra, a Venezuelan artist living in Paris.
