I like to draw simple, linear, symbolic and slightly dramatic things using fluorescent pencils. No AI. Most of these were made during the 2020–2021 lockdowns.

Every work drawn by hand. No AI.

About

I'm drawn to contradictions: fragility and the grotesque, innocence and morbidity, order and chaos, tenderness and discomfort.

I'm a self-taught amateur artist who rediscovered drawing during the 2020 lockdown, at a particularly depressing time in my life. I turn simple symbols into intricate, almost living forms. Deliberately imperfect.

As for my influences, I'm an elder millennial raised on MySpace who never entirely grew out of her emo phase or Tim Burton's early work.
